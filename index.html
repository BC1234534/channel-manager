BC Channel Manager

BC Channel Manager is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. The owner uses it to upload, schedule and follow the videos on their own YouTube channels, all channels in one place.

It is not offered to anyone else and has no sign-up.

What it does

  • Uploads a video to one of the owner's channels with its title, description, tags, settings, thumbnail and playlist, and schedules when it goes public.
  • Shows every channel's scheduled videos in one calendar.
  • Shows each channel's subscribers and each video's views, likes and comments, and reads the YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's own videos.
