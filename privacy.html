BC Channel Manager

Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers BC Channel Manager, a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels.

YouTube API Services

BC Channel Manager uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

After you sign in with Google and allow it, BC Channel Manager accesses, for each channel you connect:

  • the channel's name, subscriber count and videos, including private and scheduled ones;
  • each video's details and statistics (views, likes, comments);
  • the YouTube Analytics reports of the channel's videos, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and subscribers gained.

It uploads the videos you choose, sets their title, description, tags, settings, thumbnail, playlist and publishing time, and posts the comments you write. It does not access anything else in your Google Account.

How it stores, uses and shares it

  • Everything stays on the owner's computer: the sign-in tokens in Windows Credential Manager, the channel data in local files.
  • The data is used only to show the owner their channels and to carry out the uploads and changes the owner asks for.
  • It goes to no one except Google's YouTube APIs. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, internal or external, and not used for advertising.
  • No third party serves content or advertisements in BC Channel Manager.
  • Apart from the sign-in tokens and channel data above, it stores nothing on your device and uses no cookies or similar technology. This website uses no cookies either.

Deleting data and revoking access

  • Removing a channel in BC Channel Manager deletes its sign-in token and all data stored for it.
  • You can also revoke BC Channel Manager's access at any time on Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. BC Channel Manager checks at least every 30 days whether its access is still valid, and deletes the data of any channel whose access was revoked.
